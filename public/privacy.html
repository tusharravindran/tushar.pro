Privacy Policy

ByteLore n8n · Last updated 12 November 2026

ByteLore n8n is a personal automation run by Tushar Ravindran for the ByteLore YouTube channel. It is not a public service, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

  • Google Sheets: reads and updates one spreadsheet that lists video topics and their status.
  • Google Drive: uploads finished videos to one folder and downloads them again for publishing.
  • YouTube: uploads approved videos to the owner's own channel.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about any other person.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's self-hosted n8n instance and are used only for the actions above. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube API Services

ByteLore n8n uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

The app keeps no copy of your Google data beyond the OAuth tokens in the owner's n8n instance. To remove its access and delete those tokens:

  • Revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through the Google security settings page. Revoked tokens stop working immediately.
  • Or ask for deletion by opening an issue at github.com/tusharravindran. Stored tokens and credentials are deleted within 7 days of the request.

Spreadsheets, Drive files and YouTube videos stay in the owner's Google account and can be deleted there at any time.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
